utter 0.0.4 fixes the floor margin of 0.0.3 so that it does not cut a quiet word after a pause. Nothing else changes; with the margin unset the output is identical to 0.0.3.

## Fixed

The floor margin on the host's endpoint bound now reads no span shorter than the bound itself. In 0.0.3 a trailing `[speech]` entry or a rival's extending word was judged against the floor at any length, and the first frames of a quiet word after a pause sit near the floor on any microphone: the margin waived the veto, the bound fired between the two words of a command, and the second word came back late as short grammar words. On the first consumer's recordings that lost 29 words for 39 gained across 13 of 33 takes. A span at least the bound's length is seconds long on a cold room and a few frames at an onset, so the wordless closes stay and the onsets do not. TD-12 is amended with the rule and the measurement.
