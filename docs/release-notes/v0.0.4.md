## Fixed

The floor margin on the host's endpoint bound now reads no span shorter than the bound itself. In 0.0.3 a trailing `[speech]` entry or a rival's extending word was judged against the floor at any length, and the first frames of a quiet word after a pause sit near the floor on any microphone, so the margin could waive the veto and the bound fire into the word. A span at least the bound's length is seconds long on a cold room and a few frames at an onset, so the wordless closes stay and the onsets do not: on the public background recordings at a -50 dBFS floor the margin closes a wordless stretch at a median 1.2 s instead of 0.72, still with one forced word in 6.7 minutes where the stock rules leave 11. On the first consumer's 33 recordings, at the finals, 65 forced and floor-level phantoms leave, 9 spoken words the 20 s cap had eaten come back, and 2 short words that begin just after a wordless close are lost, the measured price of a boundary in hiss. TD-12 is amended with the rule, the measurement and the price, and a new benchmark page, quiet onsets, holds every word's first frames near the floor to keep the case in view.
